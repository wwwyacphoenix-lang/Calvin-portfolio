A little about me

Practical thinking. Purposeful building.

I’m a motivated Building Technology student developing the technical knowledge and practical experience needed for a career in the construction industry.

Who I am

I’m interested in how construction projects move from drawings to the finished building. My learning includes building plans, quantity take-off, Bills of Quantities (BOQs), and site surveying.

Through coursework and industrial attachment, I aim to strengthen my attention to detail, teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills. I’m keen to learn from experienced professionals and contribute responsibly to construction projects.

My goal is to grow into a capable construction professional and provide dependable support with measurement, documentation, and site-related tasks.
